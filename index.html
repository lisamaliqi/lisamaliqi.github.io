About

I like making the web feel good.

I trained as a front-end developer in Malmö and got here by building the things I wanted to exist — an escape-room game, a movie database, a Star Wars encyclopedia, a candy webshop, a multiplayer reaction game with its own backend.

Outside of code I spend my time on UX and interface design, and on picking up whatever's next. Most recently: solving a Rubik's Cube.
